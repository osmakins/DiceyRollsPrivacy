# DiceyRolls — Simple, Smooth, Satisfying Dice Roller

DiceyRolls is an android app that brings the feel of real tabletop dice to your phone.
With clean visuals, warm wood tones, and smooth animations, every roll feels natural and satisfying.

## ✨ Features
- Realistic dice visuals with smooth roll animation

- One or two dice modes for any game

- Roll history timeline with visual dice icons

- Warm, cozy wood‑themed interface

- Fast, lightweight, and works offline

- No ads, no tracking, no permissions required

Whether you’re playing board games, settling a debate, or just testing your luck, DiceyRolls gives you a clean, reliable dice roller you can trust.

## Privacy Policy for DiceyRolls
- DiceyRolls does not collect, store or share any personal data.      
- The app does not connect to the internet.
- The app does not use analytics or tracking tools.
- The app does not request or require any permissions.
- No data is transmitted to any server.
- All functionality runs entirely on your device.
